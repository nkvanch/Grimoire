Grimoire

A free, open-source 5e-compatible character and campaign app for Android. Build the character you actually want, keep it on your own device, and share your creations as one file.

  • Alpha 0.1.0
  • Android only
  • Works offline
  • No account, no external server
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later

Why it exists

I started Grimoire because I could not represent the character I wanted in the tools I tried. Some designs do not fit a fixed list of options: a limited resource, a custom progression, a condition, a passive effect and an active ability, all working together. Grimoire tries to model those as real data instead of a note in a text box.

I am not looking for promotion or support. I am looking for people who make things, and who will try to break it with one design that generic tools cannot handle. Even one design is plenty.

What it does today

  • Characters. Create and level a character, with a full sheet, and export or import it as a file.
  • Homebrew builders. Races, subraces, classes, subclasses, spells, items, monsters, feats, backgrounds and conditions, with a Test button in most of them so you can see what a piece of content does before you save it.
  • Sharing as one file. A .grimoire-pack can hold a species, a subclass, the spells it needs and a monster together. Anything it depends on is included automatically, and the person importing sees a preview before anything is added.
  • Custom Rule Profiles. Named sets of house rules, such as point buy, ability score caps, rest lengths and feat rules, that you can attach to a character.
  • Campaign sync. A DM hosts, players join with a room code or a QR code, over your local network. Sync is optional.
  • Offline. It works with no internet and needs no account or external server. Everything stays on your device.

What is in the app. Only content from the System Reference Document 5.1 (CC-BY-4.0) and original content. Anything else you play with is homebrew that you create or import yourself. See License and content policy.

Honest limits

  • Android only for now. There is no iOS build.
  • It is an alpha. Expect rough edges.
  • Reactive features and outcomes are shown as text and are not resolved automatically.
  • Homebrew is 5e-compatible only.
  • Campaign sync is not encrypted and has no password, so use it on a network you trust. Details are on the privacy page.
