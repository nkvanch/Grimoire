Grimoire

A free, open-source 5e-compatible character and campaign app for Android. Build the character you actually want, keep it on your own device, and share your creations as one file.

  • Alpha 0.2.2
  • Android only
  • Works offline
  • No account, no external server
  • Open source, GPL-3.0-or-later

Why it exists

I started Grimoire because I could not represent the character I wanted in the tools I tried. Some designs do not fit a fixed list of options: a limited resource, a custom progression, a condition, a passive effect and an active ability, all working together. Grimoire tries to model those as real data instead of a note in a text box.

One of the main ideas is that it stays free and open source. There is no price, no account, no ads and no tracking, and the code is public under the GPL-3.0-or-later licence, so anyone can read it, fix it or build on it. The content it ships comes from the System Reference Document, which is released under a Creative Commons licence, and everything else is something you make or import yourself.

The rules it is built on

These are the decisions I check every feature against. They are why some things are in the app and some are left out on purpose.

The table comes firstGrimoire supports the table, it does not replace it. Physical dice, miniatures, theatre of the mind and the DM's rulings all stay valid, and rolling in the app is optional.
It tracks, it does not simulateState, resources, effects and consequences are tracked. There is no battle map, no range or position tracking, and no simulation of the world. If a rule depends on distance, the table decides and the app records the result.
It says what is automatedA mechanic is either worked out by the app, tracked for you, or left to the table, and the app says which. It never pretends something is automated when it is not.
Correct before broadA smaller set of mechanics that are right is better than claiming everything with silent guesses. Features are built from reusable pieces, not one-off special cases.
Your data stays yoursIt works offline with no account. Nothing leaves your device unless you share a file or sync over your own network. A character belongs to the player, and a campaign belongs to the DM.
Content is data, not codeRules content comes as packs of plain data, and homebrew is built from the same typed pieces, never from scripts. Sharing a creation means sharing one file that you can inspect.
Clear where it came fromWhat ships in the app is SRD content under CC-BY and original work, with its source stated. Anything else is homebrew you bring yourself, so the app and this repository stay safe to distribute.
Free and open sourceNo price, no account, no ads. The source is public under GPL-3.0-or-later, so you can check every claim on this page.

What it does today

  • Characters. Create and level a character, with a full sheet, and export or import it as a file.
  • Homebrew builders. Races, subraces, classes, subclasses, spells, items, monsters, feats, backgrounds and conditions, with a Test button in most of them so you can see what a piece of content does before you save it.
  • Sharing as one file. A .grimoire-pack can hold a species, a subclass, the spells it needs and a monster together. Anything it depends on is included automatically, and the person importing sees a preview before anything is added.
  • Custom Rule Profiles. Named sets of house rules, such as point buy, ability score caps, rest lengths and feat rules, that you can attach to a character.
  • Campaign sync. A DM hosts, players join with a room code or a QR code, over your local network. Sync is optional.
  • In a browser. The same app runs in a desktop browser, which is easier for writing homebrew on a computer. Export a .grimoire-pack there and import it on your phone. Details.
  • Offline. It works with no internet and needs no account or external server. Everything stays on your device.

What is in the app. Only content from the System Reference Document 5.1 and 5.2.1 (CC-BY-4.0) and original content. Anything else you play with is homebrew that you create or import yourself. See License and content policy.

Honest limits

  • The Android app is the main build. There is no iOS build.
  • The browser version is newer and less tested: no live sessions, no PDF export, and its data lives in that browser.
  • It is an alpha. Expect rough edges.
  • Reactive features and outcomes are shown as text and are not resolved automatically.
  • Homebrew is 5e-compatible only.
  • Campaign sync is not encrypted and has no password, so use it on a network you trust. Details are on the privacy page.
