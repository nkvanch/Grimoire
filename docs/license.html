Content policy for public packs

A .grimoire-pack is yours, and you decide what goes in it. I do not host packs. If you publish one, please:

  • Include only content you made yourself, material from the SRD 5.1 (with the attribution above), or content whose author has given you permission.
  • Do not include text, stat blocks, tables or art copied from copyrighted books or products outside the SRD, or from another creator's work without their permission. Homebrew that reuses a mechanic in your own words is fine.
  • Put your name in the pack's author field, so people know who to credit.
  • Say what ruleset it is built for. Grimoire homebrew is 5e-compatible only.

Packs contain your data as plain text (JSON), so anyone can open and read one.
