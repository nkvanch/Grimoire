## What is retained

The author keeps the rights in the Grimoire name, logo and official branding. No trademark registration is claimed. This file says how the name and logo may be used, and it is written to be compatible with the GPL, which lets a project decline to grant trademark rights (GPLv3, section 7).

## What you can do

- Use, study, modify and redistribute the code under the GPL. That is the point of it.
- Say that your version is based on Grimoire, for example "Foo, based on Grimoire". Please link to this repository.
- Refer to Grimoire by name when you write about it, review it or link to it.

## What you should not do without asking

- Present a modified version as an official Grimoire release, or as made, approved or endorsed by the author.
- Use the official Grimoire logo or icon as the identity of a modified distribution. Give your version its own name and icon.
- Publish a build under the name "Grimoire" in a way that could make people think it is the official one.

If you want to do something on this list, or you are not sure, write to feedback.grimoire@gmail.com.
